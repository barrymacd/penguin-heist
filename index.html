Rules

Scoring

  • Steal: +3. A 7th grader must be present on every raid.
  • Yard photo: +1, once per date, only while you hold it. Example: steal at 11pm, snap another at 12:01am, and that's +4.
  • Caught in the act: raiders −2. No points for the catch.
  • Every point needs a photo. Caught teams, be good sports and pose.
  • Each phone joins one family with that family's PIN and can only post for that family.

What counts as caught

  • Someone from the home is physically outside, sees you and calls you out before you're in the vehicle.
  • If any raider is still outside when they're called out, the whole raid is caught.
  • Tapping on the window doesn't count. Neither does doorbell or security footage.

Display and timing

  • Front yard, displayed prominently, plugged in, inflated and lit.
  • Steals are open 24/7 unless stated below.
  • No steal-backs: if your penguin gets stolen, you can't take it back from that family on the steal date or the next date. Stolen on the 5th means you can steal it back starting 12:00am on the 7th. If another family steals it first, you're free to go after them.
  • Christmas freeze: no stealing from 10pm Christmas Eve to 6am Dec 26.
  • The game starts Nov 30 when the starting family posts the first yard photo. No steals before then.
  • The game ends at 12:00 noon on Jan 31.
